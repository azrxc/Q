Make Waqt yours

Location, calculation settings and reminders.

Location

Prayer times are calculated by AlAdhan, not official local timetables. For Malaysia, cross-check with JAKIM e-Solat. Local mosque schedules may differ.

Prayer reminders

Reminders work only while this app is open and running. Browsers may silence or suspend background pages. Audio file stays on your device for this session; no background Azan guarantee.

Install Waqt

On iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: Chrome → menu → Add to Home Screen or Install app.

Accessibility

Privacy

Location or city and settings are sent to AlAdhan to calculate times. Quran requests go to Al Quran Cloud, and mosque search sends approximate coordinates to Overpass. External links open other services. Preferences, bookmarks and journals stay in this browser. No account, analytics or advertising.

Privacy

Your selected prayer zone is sent to JAKIM or its data relay. GPS is optional for Qibla and mosque discovery. Quran requests go to Al Quran Cloud, and mosque search sends approximate coordinates to Overpass. External links open other services. Preferences, bookmarks and journals stay in this browser. No account, analytics or advertising.

# Waqt 0.5 research and implementation
- Selangor's district schedules begin 1 January 2027: https://www.muftiselangor.gov.my/takwim-solat/ and https://web26.bernama.com/radio/news.php?id=2639257 . Old SGR zones are blocked for dates from 2027; district codes MFSGR01–09 are internal SimpleSolat Data IDs, not official JAKIM codes. District monthly fallback uses Mufti Selangor-derived data at https://github.com/ragibkl/simplesolat-data ; refresh/compare against official district timetables before relying on them. If not available, fail closed.
- Optional cached geographic polygons and dated mappings: https://github.com/ragibkl/simplesolat-data . © OpenStreetMap contributors ODbL 1.0 https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright ; Rompin mukims geoBoundaries CC BY 4.0. No repackaging of data, fetched to device cache; only accept unambiguous containment, no nearest-city guess. Provider access and all special zones need real-device verification. Offline detection requires downloaded map and cached timetable.
- Quran reciters from https://alquran.cloud/cdn ; per-ayah streaming for Husary and Minshawi, full-surah stream for Alafasy, offline full-surah download where provider file is available. Personal/educational use per https://alquran.cloud/terms-and-conditions . Check commercial rights before monetizing. Audio/downloads remain on device and may be evicted by OS.
- Native home widget from stored future prayer slots; updates with alarm scheduling and periodic OS updates, not a second-by-second countdown. Add through launcher or settings. Simple mode, Hijri/imsak and iftar when source provides them. Iftar = Maghrib; Hijri from timetable, no guessed dates.
- Live official JAKIM and GPS endpoints could not be retrieved by the research browser; no claim of independent comparison. CI compilation and real-device tests still required. Future work: validate all zones against authorities, fetch current and next district month proactively, compare source discrepancies, sign releases, test web and native downloads, test widget launchers and reboot. All users should verify their zone.
